    Download settings

    Choose an existing Windows folder. Forward slashes, backslashes and surrounding quotation marks are accepted.

    Suitable repositories and databases are selected automatically. Sources include PMC, Sci-Hub, ResearchGate, Z-Library, Library Genesis, Anna’s Archive and original preprint/conference repositories. Mirror discovery and source availability are checked automatically.

    Open the publisher’s website?

    The selected sources did not retrieve a PDF automatically. Check the source history for unavailable sites or required verification. Choose a publisher link below, or search Google for other publisher options. Nothing opens until you select a link.

    The official article link resolves to the publisher or original repository (for example Nature, Cell Press, Elsevier, Wiley, ACS or RSC). You may need to sign in through your institution and download the PDF there.
